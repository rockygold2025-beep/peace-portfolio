Raw records settle into a trend. That's most of my job.

I like the moment a spreadsheet starts making sense.

Most of my work centres on sales, customer and operations data. I take it in the state it arrives in, with duplicates, gaps and entries that don't match, and get it to a point where the numbers can be trusted.

Then I look for patterns, outliers and shifts that matter to the business, and put the key metrics in one view so busy people can read it in a minute and act on it.

I'm careful with details, because one wrong filter can change the whole story. I work well independently, meet deadlines, and explain technical results in everyday language.

Next on my list: business intelligence and reporting automation.
